Written by Baoning Wu, August 22, 2025

The strong motion data of the 2023 Mw 7.8 Kahramanmaraş Earthquake used in the manuscript "Near-fault Strong-motion of the 2023 Mw7.8 Kahramanmaraş Earthquake: Insights into High-frequency Radiation Mechanisms", downloaded from the Disaster and Emergency Management Authority (AFAD), Türkiye, at https://tadas.afad.gov.tr/event-detail/17966. 

The data was downloaded on April 8, 2024. The option of "acceleration" was selected under "Raw Data" and the option of "SAC" was selected under "File Format".

Information about stations are in "data_all_summary1.csv".
